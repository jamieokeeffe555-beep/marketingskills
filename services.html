Services

From 1-to-1 sessions to group youth programmes — every service is built on professional rugby league experience and a genuine desire to see you improve.

1-to-1 Personal Training

Whether you want to get stronger, lose body fat, improve your athletic performance or simply build a consistent training habit — Ellis will design and deliver sessions built specifically around you. No cookie-cutter programmes, no generic advice. Just focused, progressive coaching with someone who has spent years in professional sport.

Each block begins with a thorough assessment of your current ability, injury history and goals. From there, Ellis plans a programme that challenges you appropriately at every stage — and checks in regularly to make sure it's working.

Rugby-Specific S&C Programmes

This is where Ellis's background gives him a genuine edge. He's not a gym coach who's read about rugby — he's a current Betfred Championship player who's trained in Super League academy environments, worked with elite S&C staff, and knows exactly what the sport demands of a body.

The programme targets the physical qualities that make rugby league players: explosive acceleration, tackle-specific strength, collision durability, aerobic base and the ability to repeat high-intensity efforts across 80 minutes. Suitable for amateur and semi-professional players at all levels.

Speed & acceleration Power development Collision conditioning Pre-season blocks In-season maintenance Injury prevention

Youth & Junior Athlete Development

Ellis knows exactly what it feels like to be a teenager with a big dream and no real roadmap. He was that kid. And he's built this service to give young athletes in the Barrow area the foundation he wished he'd had earlier — age-appropriate S&C coaching that helps them develop safely and confidently.

This isn't about chasing quick results or burning young athletes out. It's about building movement quality, athletic confidence and healthy habits that will serve them for life — whether they go on to play professionally or just want to feel stronger and more capable on and off the pitch.

Delivered with patience, encouragement and the kind of peer-level authenticity that actually gets through to young people. Ellis is 21 — he's not that far removed from where they are, and they know it.

Ages 13–18 Movement fundamentals Athletic confidence Speed & agility Safe strength development 1-to-1 and small groups

Mental Wellbeing & Performance Coaching

Professional sport teaches you a lot about pressure, identity, setbacks and resilience. Ellis has experienced all of it — leaving home at 14, chasing a dream in a big city, dealing with the demands of professional competition while still growing up. He's open about that, and he brings that openness to his coaching.

This service sits alongside all of Ellis's physical programmes, because he believes you can't separate the two. It's also available as a standalone offering for athletes and non-athletes who want support with performance mindset, managing pressure, building confidence or navigating tough periods — in sport or in life.

Ellis is not a clinical therapist, and he'll always point clients in the right direction if something is beyond his scope. But for performance mindset, goal-setting, self-belief and resilience — he's lived it, and he can help.

Performance mindset Resilience building Pressure management Goal-setting Confidence Wellbeing check-ins
