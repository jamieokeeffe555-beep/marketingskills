About Ellis

A Barrow kid who commuted the length of the country to chase a dream — and came home to inspire the next generation.

The Beginning

From Barrow to Merseyside

Ellis Archer grew up in Barrow-in-Furness, a town with rugby league in its bones and community at its heart. Like a lot of Barrow lads, rugby wasn't just a game — it was a way of life. But unlike most, Ellis had something that set him apart: an exceptional talent spotted early, and the discipline to match it.

At just 14 years old, he was picked up by St Helens — one of Super League's most decorated clubs — and brought into their scholarship and academy system. That meant leaving Barrow behind four days a week, commuting all the way to Merseyside and back, rain or shine, season after season.

Most teenagers would have cracked. Ellis didn't. For four years he made that journey without complaint, putting in the hours at one of the country's elite rugby league environments while his mates were at home doing what 14-to-18-year-olds do. That's the kind of character that defines Ellis — quiet graft, consistent effort, no excuses.

Joined fellow Cumbrian club Workington Town for the 2024–25 seasons. More first-team experience, more development, more of what makes Ellis the player he is.

2026

Re-signed with Barrow Raiders on a permanent deal for the 2026 Championship season. Three tries in his first two games back — a Championship win over Workington Town and a Challenge Cup win over Wigan St Judes — said everything about his form and his motivation. The Director of Rugby described Ellis as having "a big future." We'd agree.

The Coaching Philosophy

Graft. Community. The Whole Person.

Ellis's approach to coaching is shaped by everything he's been through — and everything he believes sport can do for people.

01

Earned, Not Given

Ellis didn't get handed anything. He commuted across the country for four years, trained when others rested, and earned every step of his career. That ethos shapes every session he runs. Results come from consistent work — no shortcuts, no passengers.

02

Community is Everything

Ellis already works within the Barrow Raiders Advantage! community programme, coaching and championing mental wellbeing in the local area. He's not here to build a brand — he's here to build people. Barrow first. Always.

03

Mind and Body Together

Professional sport taught Ellis that the mental demands are just as real as the physical ones. He's open about that, and he builds mental wellbeing into everything he does — because you can't get the best out of your body without getting the best out of your head.

In the Community

Already Giving Back

Ellis doesn't just play for Barrow Raiders — he works for the town. Alongside his playing commitments, he's already embedded in the Barrow Raiders Advantage! community programme, where he coaches and supports young people in the local area.

He's a champion for mental wellbeing, encouraging the next generation to pick up a rugby ball, get active, and talk openly about how they're feeling. In a town where opportunities aren't always plentiful, Ellis wants to be proof that dedication and belief can take you further than you think.

His coaching isn't a side hustle — it's an extension of who he is. The same values that drove a 14-year-old to commute to Merseyside week after week are the ones he brings to every session, every conversation, every young athlete he works with.

Barrow's Director of Rugby has described Ellis as having "outstanding commitment, application and attitude" — and a "big future" ahead of him. That future includes this community.
