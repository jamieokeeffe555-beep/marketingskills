The Kid From Widnes

George Delaney grew up in Widnes playing amateur rugby for Halton Farnworth Hornets. It's where most of the groundwork was laid — learning the physical demands of the game, building the competitive instincts, and developing the work ethic that would eventually take him to the top flight.

Those who've worked with George consistently describe him the same way: a lovely, laid-back kid with an almost relentless professionalism once he's in preparation mode. Not loud about it. Just quietly getting it done, day after day.

The Saints Academy Pathway

Spotted early by St Helens, George came through the full academy pathway — Scholarship level at Under-16s, progressing through to the Under-18s setup. It's one of the most demanding and respected academy programmes in Super League, and George not only completed it — he stood out in it.

He won the St Helens U18s Player of the Year award in 2022, the same year he made his first-team debut. That's not common. To be recognised at academy level while simultaneously breaking into Super League senior rugby speaks to the kind of preparation he was putting in.

Why This Matters for Your Training

George isn't coaching from a textbook. He's coaching from experience. The S&C demands of being a Super League prop — the power requirements, the conditioning loads, the recovery protocols, the in-season vs pre-season structure — these are things he lives through every week.

When George designs a programme, it's shaped by years inside one of rugby league's most professional setups. He knows what it actually takes, physically and mentally, to perform at a high level consistently. That's what he brings to his clients.

The approach isn't complicated. It's disciplined, evidence-based, and honest. George coaches the way he trains: with purpose, patience, and no shortcuts.

The Coaching Philosophy

Consistent Work
Beats Everything

George's approach to coaching comes down to one thing: sustained, intelligent effort. He doesn't believe in quick fixes or shortcuts — because in Super League, there aren't any. The players who make it, and stay at the top, are the ones who show up with the same quality every single day.

That philosophy carries into every client relationship. Training should have a clear purpose. Progression should be trackable. And the work has to match the goal. Whether you're preparing for an academy trial or simply want to be the fittest you've ever been — the standard of preparation doesn't change.
