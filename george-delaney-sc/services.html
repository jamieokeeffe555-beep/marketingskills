In-Person

1-to-1 Personal Training

In Person All Levels

One-to-one sessions with George, fully tailored to your goals, your starting point, and your schedule. Whether you're a complete beginner or a competitive athlete looking to sharpen a specific area, every session is planned and purposeful.

You won't be left to figure things out alone. George coaches technique, explains the reasoning behind each session, and adjusts the programme as you progress. The experience you get is the same quality of thinking that goes into preparing a Super League forward.

Rugby Specialist

Rugby-Specific S&C Programmes

Rugby Players Position-Specific

Rugby league is a unique physical demand — and generic gym programmes don't cut it. George builds S&C programmes grounded in the actual requirements of the game: explosive power for carries and tackles, the aerobic capacity to last 80 minutes, and the resilience to back it up week after week through a full season.

These programmes are informed by the periodisation models and training methods used at professional Super League clubs. Whether you play prop, back row, halfback, or full back, the programme is built around what your position actually demands — not a one-size-fits-all template.

  • Pre-season block building and conditioning
  • In-season maintenance and game-day performance
  • Position-specific power and speed work
  • Collision preparation and contact conditioning
  • Aerobic base and high-intensity interval work
  • Mobility and injury resilience protocols

Online Coaching & Programming

Anywhere Flexible

Not everyone can train in person — and you don't have to be local to benefit from working with George. The online coaching service delivers fully bespoke programming with ongoing support, check-ins, and accountability built in.

This isn't a downloaded PDF. George writes your programme, reviews your training videos, checks in weekly, and adjusts the plan when life gets in the way (because it will). You get the structure and the access — wherever you are.

  • Initial video call to assess goals and background

Youth Development

Youth & Academy Development Training

Under-18s Academy Pathway

George came through the academy pathway himself — scholarship level at Saints, progressing through U16s and U18s before breaking into the first team. He knows exactly what that journey demands, physically and mentally, and what the coaches at that level are looking for.

This service is for young rugby players (typically 14–18) who are serious about progressing through the academy system or want to develop the physical foundations to give themselves the best shot at being noticed. The emphasis is on building quality, sustainable athletic development — not rushing physical development at the wrong time.

  • Age-appropriate strength and power foundations
  • Speed, agility, and footwork development
  • Rugby-specific conditioning work
  • Advice on what academy coaches look for physically
  • Long-term athletic development (LTAD) principles
  • Mental preparation and professional habits
