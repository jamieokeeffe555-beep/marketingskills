The Philosophy

Training That Comes From
Actually Doing It

George Delaney made his St Helens first-team debut at 18 and hasn't stopped working since. Raised in Widnes, shaped by the Saints Academy system, and now a contracted Super League player through to 2028 — everything he teaches is grounded in what it genuinely takes to perform at elite level week after week.

This isn't generic gym content. It's sport-science-backed training built around real S&C methods George uses in his own preparation. Whether you're an aspiring rugby player or someone who simply wants to train with purpose, you'll get honest, effective coaching.
