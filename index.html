Who Is Ellis Archer?

A Barrow Lad Who Grafted His Way to the Top

Ellis Archer is a 21-year-old professional rugby league scrum-half playing for Barrow Raiders in the Betfred Championship. He's from Barrow-in-Furness — a town he loves, a town he left to chase his dream, and a town he chose to come back to.

At 14, Ellis was picked up by St Helens and spent four years commuting across the country to Merseyside, earning his place in one of Super League's top academies. In August 2022, aged just 18, he made his Super League debut against Wakefield Trinity. That's the kind of graft that shapes a coach.

Now Ellis combines his playing career with S&C coaching — sharing the lessons from a professional rugby league journey with the next generation and anyone ready to train with real purpose.
